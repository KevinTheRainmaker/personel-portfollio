# Publication summary sources

Reviewed 2026-11-14. The paper cards use concise editorial summaries, not verbatim abstracts. Their implication sentences synthesize the cited evidence and do not claim additional measured outcomes. Existing abstract fields are retained as source material; the page renders the summary field.

## Who Holds the Pen?

- Source: the manuscript abstract already present in `data/profile-data.json` at commit `44d869b4a7d035587ce74f4258c601d19cc9e438`.
- Evidence: eight speech-language pathologists, four collaboration modes, greater autonomy reducing effort but diminishing control and professional identity, and a preference for collaborative dialogue.
- Scope: no public manuscript was available for independent verification. The summary uses only the existing abstract. Under Review status, authors, date, and link remain unchanged.

## MagToTheFuture

- [First-author thesis abstract, GIST Scholar](https://scholar.gist.ac.kr/handle/local/33777)
- [HCIS Lab publication listing](https://www.hcis-lab.com/publications)
- Evidence: passive magnetic patches, external actuation, and force, vibration, and localized heating without embedded electronics. The thesis explicitly names the MagToTheFuture system; the lab confirms the UIST paper's title and authors.
- Scope: the ACM full paper was not accessible. The summary describes the documented mechanism and its design implication, without performance numbers or user-study claims. The familiar-form implication follows from adding passive patches rather than embedded powered actuators.

## PardonMix

- [Authors' institutional abstract, GIST Scholar](https://scholar.gist.ac.kr/handle/local/34467)
- Evidence: 10 engineers, 18 descriptors, and three ambiguity patterns motivating automation, degree control, and exploratory interfaces.
- Scope: the adaptive interface is a proposed design strategy. The summary does not claim that improved collaboration or preservation of intent was experimentally established.

## Demonstrating InteractiSense

- [Official CHI 2026 program abstract](https://programs.sigchi.org/chi/2026/program/content/229762)
- [ACM paper, indexed abstract and conclusion](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772363.3799141)
- Evidence: a soft tangible prototype with embedded sensing, cooperative games, and a preliminary study with 15 children examining data stability and play experience.
- Scope: direct ACM full-text retrieval was unavailable. The summary focuses on interface design and preliminary feasibility, not clinical or therapeutic effectiveness. No findings from later InteractiSense publications are attributed to this demo paper.

## LEGOLAS

- [Authors' institutional abstract, GIST Scholar](https://scholar.gist.ac.kr/handle/local/31485)
- Evidence: LLM/RAG-based verbal guidance complements visual feedback, with reported improvements in learning efficiency and self-assessment confidence without increased cognitive load.
- Scope: the full paper was unavailable. Sample sizes, test statistics, long-term retention, and equivalence to professional coaching are omitted. The previous long summary's unverified numerical claims are replaced.

## Voice-based chronic kidney disease assessment

- [Publisher full paper](https://www.thinkmind.org/articles/etelemed_2024_1_90_40029.pdf), Sections II, IV.B, and V.
- Evidence: binary classification compares CKD stages 3–5 against healthy cases and stages 1–2. The strongest reported F1 score combines health information and voice features; it is not a voice-only accuracy result.
- Scope: remote assessment is a potential application. The summary does not assert validated early-stage detection, improved clinical outcomes, or reliable longitudinal monitoring.

## A Survey on 3D Scene Graphs

- [Publisher abstract, Springer Nature](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-26889-2_13)
- Evidence: a review of definitions, generation, applicability, and future directions for representations of objects, properties, and relationships.
- Scope: the full chapter is subscription-restricted. The relational-reasoning implication is a synthesis of the survey's framing, not a new benchmark or experimentally demonstrated performance claim.
