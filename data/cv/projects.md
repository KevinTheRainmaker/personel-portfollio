## Soridam: Keeping Family Voices and Emotions Alive, Wherever You Are

**Period**: 2022

**Link**: [View Project](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrO6cmoMTF4)

Family members living in different regions and time zones may miss the tone and everyday moments that make communication feel personal. Soridam combines photos and voice messages in a family-oriented social app, with emotion-recognition-based emoji previews and an archive for revisiting familiar voices. The project explores how voice can carry emotional context and preserve shared memories when family members cannot be together.

**Tags**: Affective Computing, Emotion Recognition

## Be With You: A Soft Hug for Every Silent Struggle

**Period**: 2024

**Link**: [View Project](https://youtu.be/BK_KMwFTS7g)

Putting difficult feelings into words can be a barrier to reflecting on them. Be With You uses an LLM-based conversation guided by a psychological wellness framework to help users describe and organize their thoughts. Capybara characters and a personalized message of encouragement accompany the conversation. The project explores a gentle entry point to self-guided emotional reflection through conversation and affective feedback.

**Tags**: Emotional Reflection, Affective Design, Self-healing, LLM

![Be With You: A Soft Hug for Every Silent Struggle](/images/projects/be_with_you.png)

## Peach Seoga: Text Simplification Platform for Slow Learners

**Period**: 2024–2026

**Link**: [View Project](https://ai.peachseoga.com/)

Adapting everyday information into easy-to-read materials takes repeated work from educators and accessibility specialists. With the non-profit Peach Market, I helped develop an LLM-powered service that turns source content into accessible drafts for learners with cognitive or developmental challenges. Prompts were co-designed with experts, and users choose the output format. The service supports the drafting step so specialists can focus on reviewing and adapting materials to learners' needs.

**Tags**: Accessibility, AI for education, LLM
